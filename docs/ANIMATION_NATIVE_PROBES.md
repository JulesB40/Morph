# Animation and equipment probe contract

This document accompanies `ANIMATION_MODEL_COVERAGE_26_2.json`. The inventory explicitly lists the 91 non-Avatar living forms obtained from the pinned native `EntityTypes` fields and their entity inheritance. The model/layer names are source declarations, including renderer parents; dynamic model selection and inherited layers still require live inspection. No entry means that a model has been visually verified.

The first candidate changes copy common player pose state, drive chicken/parrot flight from a stable native-rate clock, paddle previously omitted named arms and chicken/parrot wings, and supply actual illager combat flags. Captured age/size is retained. Bat/bee flight and Wither head helpers are retained. The four underwater-walking zombie forms remain unable to swim; drowned remains a swimmer. These changes have authored native-model tests, not execution evidence in this worktree.

## Independent baseline and candidate scenarios

Run identical fixtures through Morph Lab on both loaders against a frozen baseline and candidate. Record source and source-JAR hashes, loader, artifact hash, world seed, state inputs, native/model part paths, rotations and positions, rendered model submissions, PNGs and short frame sequences. A source/helper test is component evidence; framebuffer clips establish visible motion separately. Do not use an adapter's stroke equation to generate the expected values.

1. **Common humanoid poses:** skeleton adult and captured baby zombie. Exercise crouch, passenger, gliding, spin attack and sleeping through real controls or an authoritative native setup where item controls are required. Compare render-state pose/flags to the player and native models in equivalent state; the target must retain captured baby/ageScale. Clear each pose and assert flags and bed direction reset. Native-equivalent torso rotation during elytra flight remains a separate renderer check; a copied `isFallFlying` alone cannot certify it.
2. **Illager equipment:** vindicator, pillager, evoker and illusioner; empty hands, held axe, actual swing, bow and charged/charging crossbow, both dominant hands and offhand use. Record item-layer submissions and actual hand position. Vindicator uses the native aggression gate for actual combat poses. Idle holding and ordinary food use must not fabricate aggression; evoker/illusioner must not fabricate spell casting to force an item visible. Their native conditional visibility is intentional. Actual spell action synchronization is still pending. The authored left/offhand weapon-rig correction mirrors native bow and crossbow poses. Verify held-item alignment and ensure ordinary native illagers are unchanged: only marked morph snapshots receive this correction.
3. **Native poultry wings:** adult normal/cold chicken and chick, all five parrot colors. Sample airborne stationary ticks 1 and 3, walking on the ground, takeoff and landing. Native baked right/left wings must move over time on Z with opposite signs; ground walking has no flight flap. Preserve captured parrot sitting/party poses. Sample normal/fast swimming at the same input speed and age, showing distinct strokes. Exit water and assert the original native dry pose is restored.
4. **Named arms:** iron golem, snow golem, copper golem, creaking, warden, allay and vex. Sample normal/fast swim over at least one cycle: every native arm must move and pivots/mesh scales remain unchanged. Compare dry render output before and after swimming. Preserve golem attack/flower, warden roar/sniff/emerge/dig/attack/sonic boom, creaking attack/invulnerability/death, allay dance/spin and vex charging. Existing explicit action flags are inputs; these probes do not claim their synchronization is implemented.
5. **Leg family preservation:** Sniffer and Snifflet must move all six actual `right/left_front_leg`, `right/left_mid_leg`, `right/left_hind_leg` parts. Spiders must move all eight actual named parts. Equines, camel/camel husk, llama/trader llama, rabbit, armadillo, felines, wolf, ravager, hoglin/zoglin and strider require both age models where applicable. Compare actual baked anatomy before accepting alias coverage. Adult/baby rigs may nest parts differently.
6. **Undead control:** zombie, husk, zombie villager and zombified piglin walk submerged with swim blend zero, no horizontal swim pose, no arm paddles. Drowned must enter its native horizontal swimming pose. Repeat with held items, baby variants and water entry/exit; armor layer and base skeleton must agree.
7. **Existing flight/aquatic controls:** retain bat/bee takeoff/hover, Wither side-head facing and six-leg Sniffer passing evidence. The authored bee swimmer uses the actual native joined `front_legs`, `middle_legs`, `back_legs` meshes in both adult/baby rigs, while retaining wing motion; verify all three over time at normal and fast speed. Aquatic species each require normal/fast water movement, hover, vertical movement and water exit. Native aquatics must never receive generic limb overrides. Turtle's quadruped superclass does not make it a generic quadruped swimmer.
8. **Frozen native clocks/gates:** Breeze native `tick()` unconditionally starts idle alongside its action clocks. The detached adapter now starts only this idle at a stable epoch; baked native rods/head/wind motion must advance across fresh states, while shoot/slide/inhale/jump remain unchanged. Creaking native model applies the player's copied walk phase only when `canMove`; the authored adapter enables this solely when that phase has positive speed and the player is alive. Compare its actual leg motion while walking, then stop and die, retaining independent native action clocks. This changes rendering only and does not grant movement or heart linkage. Phantom source already derives flap time from adapter tick count and ID offset; verify the existing native phase before labeling it broken.
9. **Dragon wings:** native `EnderDragon.aiStep` records the previous flap and advances by `0.2 / (horizontalSpeed * 10 + 1) * 2^verticalSpeed` while freely flying; the renderer interpolates previous/current. The authored helper retains that formula for ordinary velocity, uses the native 0.1 resting rate on player ground contact without setting boss phase flags, and freezes on player death. It keeps one clock per detached adapter, advances once per player tick, interpolates partial ticks, wraps periodic phase, and caps missed-render catch-up at five ticks. Extreme vertical inputs are bounded to +/-16 before exponentiation and the result to four cycles/tick. Test actual native wings/tips across fresh states, hover/travel/ascent/descent/ground samples, repeated extraction of the same tick/frame, phase wrap, death, tick rollback and long render gaps. Framebuffer proof of wing visibility and independent actor/observer clocks remains required; this does not establish native dragon navigation, attacks, sitting, landing or wall interactions.

## Coverage that remains open

Run per-form extraction, visible appearance, normal/fast water motion, dry walk/look, equipment applicability and variant-model smoke probes for every inventory row. Broad family membership never replaces this form-level evidence. The following need additional adapters or reviewed explicit applicability: limbless/cube body strokes, breeze action states, dragon wing/history control, phantom phase and size, armadillo roll, rabbit native hop/idle, copper golem actions, equine rearing/eating, panda actions and the other captured/action states listed in the audit. A limb without a native anatomical counterpart is explicitly inapplicable; motion of the body/native effect may still be required.

Player main/offhand and head/chest/legs/feet drive live ordinary equipment where native layers support it. BODY/SADDLE are separate captured appearance slots and must not create transferable inventory. Their authored application rebuilds display damage/dye/glint, validates the native Equippable slot and allowed entity types, and clears missing gear. Unit coverage uses native wolf armor's direct allowed-entity holders; tag-based saddles/horse armor/harnesses require an actual loaded tag fixture. The initial descriptor capture currently covers sheep/slime rather than rideable gear, so end-to-end gear capture and rendered saddle/body-layer checks remain open. Confirm descriptor switching clears old gear, unsupported items/species stay empty, native layers align in normal/fast swim, and the player's inventory never gains captured gear.

No detached entity AI tick, false spell/attack flag, fabricated held item, defaulted visible gear or changed movement rule may be used to make an animation probe pass.
